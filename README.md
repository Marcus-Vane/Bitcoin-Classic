Philosophy 项目理念

Today, almost everyone has heard of Bitcoin.  
However, very few people have ever truly experienced acquiring Bitcoin through mining.

如今，几乎所有人都听说过比特币，  
但真正通过“挖矿”方式获得比特币的人却少之又少。

Bitcoin Classic aims to bring that original experience back.  
We make mining accessible again, allowing everyone to participate and enjoy the process.

比特币怀旧版旨在还原这种最初的体验，  
让每个人都可以参与挖矿，感受挖矿带来的乐趣与成就感。

This is not just a blockchain — it is a return to the roots of Bitcoin.

这不仅仅是一条区块链，更是一种回归比特币本源的尝试。

---

**This is a new beginning.**  
**这是一个新的开始。**
